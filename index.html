02 / ABOUT

Hi, I'm Tig.

I'm Tiago — Tig everywhere else. I'm sixteen, and I've spent over two years editing short-form and long-form videos, obsessing over the things that stop a viewer from scrolling away: rhythm, hooks and the small motion touches that make a cut feel alive.

Every project is a story problem, not a pile of effects. If it doesn't hold attention, it gets cut.
